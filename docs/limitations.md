# Limitations

- The held-out evaluation covers one season and 20 clubs.
- Associated-season PPG is not a perfectly post-deadline target because league play can begin
  before the summer transfer window closes.
- The post-window-close target and other sensitivity analyses are post-hoc, not independent
  validation.
- Published fee values may be reported or estimated, and missing fees remain unknown.
- Known-fee transfer balance excludes wages, amortization, agent fees, bonuses, payment timing,
  conditional payments, and other accounting items.
- Aggregate window totals do not encode player quality, roles, minutes, replacement quality,
  transfer type, contract context, or realistic alternative windows.
- The observational forecast design does not identify causal transfer effects.
- The conceptual Pareto frontier was not empirically estimated.
- The project does not validate an optimizer, an optimal sale amount, a safe-to-sell threshold, or
  an alternative-window scenario generator.
- Raw and row-level data are excluded pending separate rights review, limiting independent
  end-to-end reproduction.
